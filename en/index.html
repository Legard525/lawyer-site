About

Twenty-five years in Russian courts

I have handled cases in Moscow courts since 2001. Judges change, so do opposing parties, and the codes keep being amended, but one thing stays constant: cases are won by thorough preparation. Knowing the case file well, you can read correctly what is happening in the courtroom and what the judge means when addressing the parties, and so react in time and in the right way to secure a favourable decision for my client.

Although Russian law lets you run a civil case on your own, an attorney means you don't have to attend long hearings that are hard both emotionally and legally. With me as your representative, you also don't have to negotiate with the other side yourself. On your instructions I study the situation, build the legal position and represent your interests in court under a power of attorney.

Practice areas

I handle a case in full: from reviewing documents and building the legal position to hearings at every level of court.

Housing disputes

Eviction and reinstatement, recognition of ownership and residence rights, division of an apartment and buy-out of a minor share, life-annuity contracts, removing obstacles to living in your home.

Inheritance

Establishing acceptance of an inheritance, restoring a missed deadline, contesting wills, dividing an estate, recognising ownership by inheritance.

Family matters

Divorce, including without your attendance at hearings, division of marital property, child and spousal support, including as a fixed sum, children's residence and contact arrangements.

Courts & representation

Handling the case at every level of court, including appeals at the Moscow City Court, negotiations with the other side and settlement agreements.

Also: consumer protection, employment disputes, debt recovery, compensation for damage, including moral damage (road accidents, flooding, dog bites), contract review, criminal defence including parole, representation of victims.

How I handle a case

01

First conversation

We briefly discuss your situation by phone, messenger or at the office. You learn whether the case has prospects and where to start.

02

Agreement

We sign a legal services agreement that sets out the tasks, the way we work and the fee.

03

Position and documents

I study the documents, gather evidence, build the legal position and prepare the claim, objections or appeal.

04

Court

I represent you at hearings under a power of attorney and report on every step — through to the judgment and the writ of execution.

A power of attorney for the case takes one visit to a notary. After that I can represent you in court without you having to attend.
